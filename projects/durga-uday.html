The houses

Eight houses on 7.5 × 15 m plots

Each house is planned on a plot of 7.5 by 15 metres. Seven are 2 BHK and one is 3 BHK. The 2 BHK ground floor has a living room, two bedrooms, a kitchen with a pooja unit, two toilets, a sitting area at the back, and parking for a car at the front.

A staircase beside the front verandah leads up into the glazed tower, which gives the street its row of tall, lit frontages.
